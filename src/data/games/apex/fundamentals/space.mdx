## what is space?

**space** is the playable area a team controls: the ground, cover, and sightlines that they can fight from *safely*. The more **space** you own, the more options you have to deal damage, avoid damage, and gather info.

understanding how to gain a **space advantage** is what I consider to be the most important skill in apex! Few people talk about how space explicitly works, since on the surface "just gaining more space than your opponent" seems like a very intuitive idea. 

however, I notice a lot of mid-ranked players (gold to low diamond) overlook or ignore space management, often relying on instinct instead of consciously controlling it. With a bit more active thought about space, they can improve their gameplay **significantly**.

in this writeup, I'll be explaining how space works in apex, and how you can manipulate space to your advantage.

### geometric and geographic understanding of the map

**geometric:** micro knowledge, cover specific, what angles am i exposed from in this specific spot?

accessing angles in which the opponent doesn't have cover (building climb spots on storm point and king's canyon)

when pushing up, pathing in such a way that your opponent can't tell where you are

**geographic:** bigger picture macro knowledge, what buildings are good vs bad, understanding the map

utilizing flanks / wraps / wide swings

| **pred player**                                                                                                              | **noob**                                                       |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| superior map knowledge, experience to put themselves in superior angles (superior cover), aka "angling out" their opponents. | poor understanding of the map, both geometric and geographic   |
| minimizing the angles they can get shot from while maximizing the space they control and can gain info from                  | hiding and not getting info, allowing themselves to be wrapped |
| short, headglitch peeks                                                                                                      | long, full body peeks                                          |
